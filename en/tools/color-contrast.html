About this tool

Free online color contrast checker. Check contrast ratio against WCAG 2.2 standards to ensure your website or app is readable by everyone, including users with visual impairments.

Supports HEX, RGB, HSL color input. Real-time contrast ratio display with automatic AA (4.5:1 normal / 3:1 large) and AAA (7:1 normal / 4.5:1 large) compliance checking.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WCAG contrast standard?

WCAG defines minimum contrast ratios: AA requires 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text; AAA requires 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.

How is contrast ratio calculated?

Contrast ratio = (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05) where L1 is lighter color luminance, L2 is darker. Relative luminance uses sRGB color space.

When is AA standard required?

AA is the minimum legal accessibility requirement for most websites and apps. At least AA compliance is recommended.

What if contrast fails?

Darken foreground or lighten background to improve ratio. Dark text on light backgrounds typically works best.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All processing is done locally in your browser. Color data never leaves your computer.
