IP Address Lookup

Look up your IP address, geolocation, ISP details, and network information

🔒 Privacy: the lookup runs in your browser against the public ip-api.com endpoint. We do not store your IP address or any lookup history.

About this tool

The IP Address Lookup tool instantly shows your current public IP address along with geolocation data derived from that IP, including country, region/state, city, ZIP code, latitude, longitude, and timezone. It also displays the network operator (ISP), organization, and Autonomous System Number (ASN), helping you understand where your connection originates or troubleshoot network, proxy, and VPN issues.

You can also enter any IP address in the input box to look it up. The tool queries the free public ip-api.com endpoint, and all requests are made directly from your browser — we never store your IP address or lookup history. Results can be copied with one click and the approximate location can be opened on a map, which is handy for debugging, log analysis, or checking where a server is hosted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool store my IP address?

No. The lookup is made directly from your browser to the public ip-api.com endpoint. We do not log or store your IP address or any lookup history.

Why is the city or location inaccurate?

IP geolocation is estimated from provider-assigned databases. It is usually accurate to the city level but can be off, especially on mobile networks, VPNs, or proxies. It is not your exact physical address.

Can I look up someone else's IP address?

Yes, enter any public IP address (e.g. 8.8.8.8) to see its location and network operator. Private IPs (e.g. 192.168.x.x) cannot be resolved.

What if the lookup fails or returns nothing?

The free API may rate-limit HTTPS requests, so wait a moment and try again. It could also be a network issue or a browser extension blocking the request.

What are ASN and ISP?

The ISP is the operator that provides your internet access. The ASN (Autonomous System Number) uniquely identifies an independent network on the internet and is often used for routing and origin analysis.
