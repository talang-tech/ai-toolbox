Word Counter

Real-time character, word, line, and paragraph counter

About this tool

Free online word counter — real-time statistics for characters (with/without spaces), words, lines, and paragraphs. Supports mixed Chinese and English text. Perfect for essay writing, blog posts, and social media character limits (Twitter/X, etc).

All counting happens locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded.

Key Features

  • Real-time count of Chinese characters, English words
  • Count lines and paragraphs
  • Support mixed Chinese and English
  • Large text paste support
  • Browser local processing, no data upload

Frequently Asked Questions

Are spaces counted as characters?

Yes, spaces are counted as characters. Multiple consecutive spaces are also counted.

Are punctuation marks counted?

Punctuation is counted in character count, but usually not in word count.

How large text is supported?

Any size text that fits in browser memory can be processed.
