Frequently Asked Questions

What is a barcode?

A barcode is a method of encoding data in a visual pattern of black and white stripes, widely used in product management, inventory tracking, and logistics.

What's the difference between Code128 and EAN-13?

Code128 supports alphanumeric and special characters for internal use. EAN-13 is a 13-digit international standard for retail product identification.

How do I verify barcode correctness?

Standard barcodes like EAN-13 and UPC-A include check digits, which the tool calculates automatically. Code128 also includes a checksum for scan accuracy.

Can I use generated barcodes commercially?

Yes. The generated barcodes follow international standard formats and can be used commercially. Ensure your data content complies with applicable laws.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All processing is done locally in your browser using Canvas. Your data never leaves your computer.
