Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between binary and decimal mode?

Binary (1024-base) uses 1 KB = 1024 Bytes, standard in OS and memory. Decimal (1000-base) uses 1 KB = 1000 Bytes, often used by hard drive manufacturers.

How many MB in 1 GB?

Binary: 1 GB = 1024 MB. Decimal: 1 GB = 1000 MB.
