UUID Generator

Generate UUID v4 / v7 (standard format)

About this tool

UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) is a 128-bit identifier with extremely low collision probability, commonly used as database primary keys and distributed system IDs.

Supports v4 (fully random) and v7 (timestamp-based, sortable). Generate multiple at once.
