About this tool

Detect and decode QR codes from image files. Supports JPG, PNG, etc. All processing happens locally in browser.

Key Features

  • Upload image to decode QR code
  • JPG/PNG/GIF format support
  • Multiple QR codes recognition
  • One-click copy decoded result
  • Pure frontend no upload

Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, GIF and other common image formats.

Are images uploaded to server?

No. Image decoding happens entirely in your browser.

Can it read damaged QR codes?

Damaged codes within error correction tolerance can be read.
