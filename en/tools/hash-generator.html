Frequently Asked Questions

Is MD5 still secure?

MD5 is cryptographically broken, not suitable for passwords, but still widely used for file checksums.

Is hashing encryption?

No. Hashing is one-way digest, original data cannot be recovered. Encryption is two-way and decryptable.

What is hash collision?

Two different inputs producing the same hash. Good hash algorithms have extremely low collision probability.
