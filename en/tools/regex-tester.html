Frequently Asked Questions

Is regex case-sensitive?

Yes by default. Use i flag to ignore case.

How to match any character?

Use . to match any character except newline. Add s flag to include newlines.

What's the difference between greedy and non-greedy?

Greedy matches as much as possible; non-greedy (with ?) matches as little as possible.
