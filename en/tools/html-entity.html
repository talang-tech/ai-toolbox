Frequently Asked Questions

Why escape HTML characters?

< > & " ' have special meaning in HTML and must be escaped to display correctly.

What's the difference between named and numeric entities?

Named entities like < are readable; numeric entities like < have wider coverage.

Do Chinese characters need escaping?

Generally not needed in UTF-8 encoding.
