Unix Timestamp Converter

Convert between Unix timestamps (seconds/ms) and human-readable dates

About this tool

Unix timestamp is the number of seconds (or milliseconds) since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC, the most common time representation in programming.

Supports: auto-detect seconds/ms, Beijing Time, UTC, ISO 8601 format conversion.

Key Features

  • 10-bit second timestamp conversion
  • 13-bit millisecond timestamp conversion
  • Real-time current timestamp display
  • Local time / UTC display
  • ISO 8601 format support
  • Millisecond precision

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Unix timestamp?

Unix timestamp is the number of seconds (or milliseconds) since Jan 1, 1970 UTC 00:00:00.

Why 10 digits vs 13 digits?

10 digits = second precision, 13 digits = millisecond precision.

What is the Year 2038 problem?

32-bit signed integers will overflow on Jan 19, 2038. Most systems now use 64-bit timestamps.
