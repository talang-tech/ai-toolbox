Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get the color value?

Click the color picker on the left, or click a preset swatch. HEX/RGB/HSL/HSV values appear on the right — click 📋 to copy.

What's the difference between HEX, RGB, HSL?

HEX is hexadecimal notation; RGB is red/green/blue components; HSL is hue/saturation/lightness, more intuitive for humans.

Is my data processed locally?

Yes, all color processing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
