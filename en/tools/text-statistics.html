Text Statistics Analyzer

Deep text analysis: characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading time, word frequency, readability score

About this tool

Free online text statistics analyzer that goes beyond basic word count. Real-time analysis: character count (with/without spaces, CJK/alphabetic), word count, sentence count, paragraph count, estimated reading time, top frequent words, word length distribution, and readability scores.

All analysis happens locally in your browser. Perfect for writers, editors, students, and SEO professionals.

Key Features

  • Real-time char count (total/no-space/CJK/alphabetic)
  • Word count, sentence count, paragraph count
  • Estimated reading time (minutes)
  • Top frequent word ranking with adjustable min word length
  • Word length distribution chart
  • Readability score (Flesch / Chinese simplicity ratio)

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference from Word Counter?

Word Counter does basic counting; this tool provides deep analysis like word frequency, reading time, and readability scores.

Does it support Chinese text?

Yes. Chinese text is analyzed by characters and words. Mixed Chinese-English text is supported.

How does word frequency work?

It counts occurrences and shows the top 20 most frequent words, with minimum word length filtering.

Is data uploaded to server?

No. All analysis is done locally in your browser.

How accurate is sentence detection?

It splits on punctuation marks (. ? ! ...). Accuracy is high for well-punctuated text.
