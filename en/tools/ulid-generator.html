About this tool

Free online ULID generator. ULID (Universally Unique Lexicographically Sortable Identifier) is a better alternative to UUID—they are sortable by time, use Crockford Base32 encoding (shorter than UUID), and present a case-insensitive-friendly string representation.

A 26-character ULID consists of a 10-character timestamp (millisecond precision, 48-bit) and 16 random characters. Custom timestamp, batch generation (1-100), sort by timestamp, copy, and download. All processing is local.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between ULID and UUID?

ULIDs are lexicographically sortable; UUID v4 is not. ULIDs use Crockford Base32 (26 chars), shorter than UUID's 36. ULIDs are uppercase-only and case-insensitive-friendly. They encode a millisecond-precision timestamp that can be decoded back to a date.

What are ULIDs good for?

Distributed database primary keys, message queue IDs, log tracing IDs, time-series sorting, any scenario needing unique+sortable IDs.

Are ULIDs random enough?

ULIDs have 80 bits of randomness (~1.2x10^24 combinations), making collisions extremely unlikely for most distributed use cases.

Can I decode the timestamp from a ULID?

Yes. The first 10 characters encode a millisecond-precision timestamp. Decode the Base32 to get the millisecond epoch value.

Is my data uploaded?

No. ULIDs are generated entirely locally in your browser with no network requests.
